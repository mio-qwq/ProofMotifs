# Research roadmap

Updated: 2026-10-18. No unified new theory is presumed.

## Priorities

1. SC-001: exact moment order under additive convolution. Establish the characteristic-free coefficient identity, equality criterion, and a smallest positive-characteristic failure. Compare with divided-power algebra and Lucas congruences. This is a calibration task, not a presumptively original theorem.
2. SC-002: saturated mixed differences and finite additive subspaces. Study how ordinary polynomial order differs from the additive-group filtration. Compare with subspace polynomials, Moore determinants and functional-degree calculus before assigning novelty.
3. SC-003: characterize which extra data beyond two scalar orders determines cancellation after composition. Work in explicitly fixed finite fields first. Investigate full moment profiles and carry-free multiplication, with sharpness or impossibility certificates.
4. PT-001: only after a useful theorem exists, test a genuinely held-out application. Previously read MUB material is an external calibration case, not an unseen or human-authored control.

## Milestones

Near term: reproducible source cards for three distinct mechanisms and one withdrawn argument; exact arithmetic tests with positive/negative cases; one complete written calibration proof and adversarial challenges; restoreable Git checkpoints.

Medium term: assess finite-characteristic filtration/product questions against functional-degree and modular group-algebra literature; implement and kernel-check a minimal Lean core when a compiler is available; obtain genuinely independent review; preregister a neutral sample before assessing AI/human prevalence.

Long term: seek a nontrivial theorem or counterexample with documented distance from prior work and a new application. Expand beyond cancellation only after comparing evidence and expected value.

## Decision gates

Do not spend multiple cycles renaming kernels or annihilators. If known algebra directly implies a candidate, classify it as Level 0 and reuse it as infrastructure. If scalar orders fail, preserve the counterexample and determine the missing invariant. If two cycles add no verifiable information, narrow the statement or change to a neighboring tractable question. No novelty or transfer claims without source-specific comparison and a new target.

## Empirical study

The targeted startup cases cannot estimate prevalence. A future 20-family / 10-control sample must be selected before mechanism annotation, from a pinned complete catalogue, with discipline matching, exclusion logs, family-level units, and held-out targets. Verify actual authorship and AI involvement of controls. No statistics or coverage percentages are promised by the startup sample.

## Update after the generator-row cycle

SC-007 reduces the defect-value bound to 2m-3 with a supplied power basis. A transvection adversary proves floor(m/2) queries necessary for deterministic decision from Q values alone. The asymptotic order is therefore linear in that restricted oracle model. Do not continue searching for a quadratic lower bound or imply a lower bound for direct access to T: its m basis images already determine it.

The next gate is a value and prior-art review of this exact oracle problem, rather than polishing constants indefinitely. Investigate whether useful additional structure remains when additivity is removed, beginning with the unavoidable independent sign choices on opposite pairs. Any proposed arbitrary-permutation classification must account for those symmetries and should be compared with finite-geometric and quadratic-residue preserver results. Until examined, that is a research target, not an accepted theorem. Keep actual formalization and a genuinely new application as medium-term alternatives.

## Update after the arbitrary-preserver quotient cycle

The independent-sign obstruction is now proved. Arbitrary preservers do not become additive: the exact group is a sign kernel over the automorphism group of the 3-uniform zero-sum hypergraph on nonzero squares. F_9 and F_27 are classified exactly, while the general semilinear quotient statement remains PM-CONJECTURE-0002.

The SC-007 prior-art/value gate is closed: retain the deterministic exact Q-only Theta(m) result as a supporting proposition, not a standalone optimal-testing theorem. No further constant optimization is planned without a natural application.

The full F_81 quotient automorphism group is now computed exactly: all 160 automorphisms are square-scalings composed with Frobenius. Together with F_27 this verifies the conjecture in degrees 3 and 4, but does not prove it generally. Immediate priority: obtain a scalable F_243 certificate or a structural theorem through restricted Freiman automorphisms and cyclotomic/additive design literature. The projective square embedding is already present in Buratti-Nakic (2024), so novelty can only lie in the size-three relation, its automorphism classification, or a new cancellation application.

Stop or redirect after two cycles if the general group is covered by known design theory or no verifiable structural invariant emerges. Preserve the quotient theorem and negative sign example as useful boundary results. Formalization and a genuinely held-out application remain medium-term alternatives.
