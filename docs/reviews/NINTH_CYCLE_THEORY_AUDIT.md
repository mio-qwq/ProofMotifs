Task scope: read-only ProofMotifs repository and temporary mathematical notes. No shared repository edits or pushes. Assumptions: m >= 3, K=F_(3^m), S=(K*)^2=<gamma>, N=(3^m-1)/2. E={1,a,b} is a distinct zero-sum triple in S. Let J(E) have all edges tE, t in S.

## Component theorem

Put H=<a,b> <= S, h=|H|, delta=[S:H], and F_3(H)=F_(3^d). Then d divides m and the incidence kernel of J(E) over F_3 has dimension at least delta(d+1). In particular if the kernel equals the (m+1)-dimensional affine restriction space, H must equal S. Equivalently gcd(i,j,N)=1 if a=gamma^i and b=gamma^j.

Proof: every tE is contained in tH, and its three vertices give adjacency steps by a and b. Starting from t, the paths through edges sE allow multiplication and division by a and b. The connected components are therefore precisely the delta cosets tH. Multiplying by t identifies each component with the seed-orbit hypergraph on H.

The F_3-linear span V of H is closed under multiplication because products of generators h_1,h_2 remain in H. It contains 1 and is a finite subring of K, hence is the subfield F_3(H)=F_(3^d). Thus dim V=d.

For c in F_3 and linear ell:F_(3^d)->F_3, the function x |-> c+ell(x) on H satisfies every component edge equation, since 3c+ell(u+v+w)=0. These d+1 functions are independent on H: H is nontrivial, so choose h_0!=1 in H; multiplication by h_0 permutes H and yields (h_0-1)*sum_H x=0, hence sum_H x=0. If c+ell(x)=0 for all H, summing gives h*c=0. Because h divides 3^m-1, h is nonzero in F_3, hence c=0. Since H spans F_(3^d), ell=0. The delta independently supported component spaces establish the lower bound.

It remains to prove strictness for H proper. If d=m, delta>=2 and delta(d+1)>=2(m+1)>m+1. Otherwise put r=m/d>=2. As h<=3^d-1,

delta >= (3^m-1)/(2(3^d-1)) = (1+3^d+...+3^((r-1)d))/2 >= r.

For the final inequality 3^(jd)>=(1+2j), so the sum is at least r^2>=2r. Therefore delta(d+1)>=r(d+1)=m+r>m+1.

## Producing a nonlinear kernel function from an extra root

Suppose k modulo N is outside {0,3^j:0<=j<m} and satisfies p(gamma^k)=0, where p=1+Z^i+Z^j. Any scalar c in K gives

f_c(x)=Tr_(K/F3)(c*x^k), x in S,

and its three values sum to zero on all tE, because the sum equals Tr(c*t^k*(1+a^k+b^k))=0. If k has Frobenius orbit of length m, c=1 gives a nonzero non-affine function: its character expansion is the sum of the m distinct characters x^(k*3^r mod N), each with coefficient one. Character independence follows from the fact that a nonzero polynomial of degree <N cannot vanish on all N distinct elements of S. Its frequencies are disjoint from the affine spectrum {0,3^j}, so it is outside that space. If the orbit has length r<m, c must instead be chosen with nonzero relative trace Tr_(K/F_(3^r))(c); otherwise the absolute trace expression can vanish identically when 3 divides m/r. That qualification must not be omitted.

There is also a more elementary proof of nonaffinity. Since -1 belongs to S and 34 is even, f(-u)=f(u).
