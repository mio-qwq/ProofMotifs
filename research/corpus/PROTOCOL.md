# Corpus protocol

The startup index contains targeted source cards for three mathematically different cancellation mechanisms, an already-read external MUB calibration case, and a withdrawn proof. It is NOT a representative sample and cannot support prevalence estimates or AI-versus-human comparisons.

A later empirical sample should use a frozen complete family catalogue and select 20 family units without cancellation-keyword filtering, with discipline-stratified allocation and a recorded deterministic seed. Select 10 comparators in matched subject areas and verify their authorship/AI involvement rather than assuming that any non-OpenAI repository is human-authored. Record exclusions before mechanism coding. Separate pilot, development and held-out transfer sets; the previously read MUB case belongs to calibration, not the holdout.

For every source keep its URL, revision, blob hash where available, inspected range, exact theorem assumptions, upstream correction status, verification scope and redistribution policy. No full third-party manuscript or Lean library is republished here. Do not label an upstream theorem correct merely because source code exists. A neutral sampling manifest is not yet complete, so hypotheses H1-H5 remain untested at population level.
