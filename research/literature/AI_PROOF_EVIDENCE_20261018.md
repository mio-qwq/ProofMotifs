## Answer the evidence can presently support

There are concrete, load-bearing cancellation mechanisms in several inspected AI-attributed proof artifacts. Their mathematical roles are demonstrable locally. This is much weaker than showing that current AIs solve research mathematics unusually well because of cancellation. The release is a selected collection from one unreleased model, not an experiment measuring present publicly accessible systems. Neither prevalence, human-versus-AI enrichment, nor causal dependence of model success on cancellation has been established.

The useful research question is conditional and testable: on independently specified problem families admitting a particular cancellation certificate, do models discover or exploit that certificate more effectively, and does exposing an explicit certificate or transfer lemma improve validated outcomes beyond a library of the existing classical methods?

## What could explain model performance, and how little these data identify

There are at least three compatible hypotheses: broad retrieval/recombination of established techniques; enough search/compute to find a rigid auxiliary structure; and reliable local symbolic or formal verification once a certificate is found. The inspected examples make all three plausible hypotheses. They do not distinguish them.

A proof's written mechanism and a model's discovery mechanism are different objects. Even a mathematically indispensable cancellation identity does not prove that the model found its proof by explicitly recognizing cancellation. Abridged reasoning summaries can suggest candidate steps but are selected, incomplete process evidence. The README contains summaries for ten families, not a full attempt trace denominator.

Release selection S may depend on apparent correctness/significance, formalizability, discipline and problem fame. Analyses conditional on S=1 can therefore create or exaggerate correlations among cancellation, checking and success. Post-hoc coding of a successful proof's mechanism cannot measure whether a task offered cancellation opportunities before the attempt. Correlation among accepted artifacts does not establish that supplying a cancellation tool improves success.

## Neutral study that can actually be done

1. Freeze and reconcile the frame. The inventory here independently confirms the current 372 family labels and 719 manuscript rows. It is NOT a representative sample. It records metadata and exposure only; discipline labels have not been assigned. Obtain discipline labels from the frozen overview or a documented coding rule. Add a separate frozen withdrawn/repaired dependency supplement. Do not count the 749 preprints directories returned by the contents API as manuscripts: that includes archived versions and other catalogue discrepancies.

2. Keep the project protocol's exploratory scale: 20 randomly selected AI-attributed families, discipline-stratified with a recorded seed, and 10 human comparators matched in subject, theorem/proof genre, statement complexity, period and verification opportunity. Two AI families per human comparator is a group-matched exploratory design, not 20 independent paired controls. Verify comparator authorship and possible AI assistance. Sample without cancellation terms, include unavailable/uncertain cases in the accounting, and record exclusions before mechanism coding. Dependency clusters, not just manuscript titles, determine independence.

3. Pre-register a narrow multilabel rule. A positive structural-cancellation code needs (i) a specified sum/integral/coefficient or signed combination, (ii) a certified vanishing/selection/scale gain, (iii) a structural reason such as involution, characteristic, orthogonality or moment annihilation, and (iv) a cited dependency path showing a nontrivial proof consequence. Routine subtraction of both sides and arbitrary A−B=0 rewrites are excluded. Record exact/approximate, order, scalar field, certificate availability, theorem role, other bottlenecks and uncertain status. A local positive can be established sooner than a family negative.

4. Have two blinded readers independently code proof passages with author/model provenance removed when feasible. Show disagreements and unknowns; do not force every family into a confident positive/negative. Keep artifact checking status distinct. Report the fraction with intervals within the released frame and matched-control differences as exploratory descriptive results. Twenty families will give wide uncertainty; no population or superiority headline is warranted.

5. Estimate actual success only with attempts. For prospective evaluation, select fresh problem units and annotate opportunity/difficulty before solving. Use the same public model/version, context, tool access and time/token budget, retain every attempt, and validate exact conclusions. The historical unreleased model cannot be reconstructed from this repository. A current-model result would be a new experiment, not a retrospective explanation of this release.

6. Test utility with randomized assistance. On matched unseen tasks, compare ordinary access to classical lemmas, an explicit existing certificate library, and the proposed new abstraction, with equal information and compute. Include both cancellation-friendly and contrast tasks, deliberately false premises, and boundary cases (degree q−1; nonprimitive roots; lost no-wrap; nonzero mass; lost join closure; observer visited between checkpoints). Primary outcome: independently validated complete solutions including correct rejection of false claims. Secondary outcomes: resource cost, verified intermediate lemmas and hypothesis errors. A study testing a new abstraction must baseline the strongest relevant existing methods; comparison against no help only measures the value of help.

## Does a new tool appear necessary?

No necessity is established. The inspected identities are already explained by finite-field power sums, character orthogonality, Newton interpolation, mean-zero test subtraction, involutions and classical geometric/analytic machinery. A universal statement that all conclusions are cancellation has too little discrimination to explain capability.

A potentially useful contribution would be a typed certificate/transfer framework that preserves the right scalar arithmetic, symmetry, moment order, regularity and closure hypotheses, proves a quantitative or structural result not assumed in the input, and discovers or verifies something on a genuinely unexamined object. Its empirical claim should be reduced hypothesis error or increased validated task success compared with an equally informative existing-tool library. Packaging kernels or renaming zero sums is insufficient.

The fastest actionable next test is a blinded local audit of five certificates: the finite-field/regular-incidence compatibility in 197; mean-zero scale gain in 085; period/no-wrap/square filtering in 107; triangle exclusion plus the independent matching bottleneck in 157; and all-time observer exclusion in 376. Ask reviewers to reconstruct each exact dependency and its smallest failed-hypothesis example. This calibrates the codebook and tests whether the proposed language reveals missing assumptions before spending effort on a new general theory. Then freeze the codebook and sample the 20+10 exploratory corpus.
